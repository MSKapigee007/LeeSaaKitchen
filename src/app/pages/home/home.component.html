About LeeSa's GRILL

Indo-American Fusion in Augusta, GA

LeeSa’s Grill is an Indo-American fusion restaurant in Augusta, GA, offering delicious and wholesome NutriBowls, sandwiches, pizzas and more. We provide daily meal plans and convenient doorstep delivery. Our catering services are perfect for parties, corporate events, celebrations and gatherings, with exciting live stations featuring BBQ, Indian chaat and pani puri. Craving authentic Indian food? Order traditional favorites ranging from breakfast tiffins like idly and dosa to flavorful biryani, haleem, curries and more. Whether dining in, ordering delivery or planning an event, LeeSa’s Grill brings fresh, flavorful food to your table.
